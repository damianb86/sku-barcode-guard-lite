# Product brief

## Problema
Shopify does not fully enforce unique merchant identifiers across every operational workflow.

## Usuario objetivo
Ops lead managing large catalogs, ERP/POS syncs or frequent product imports.

## Merchant persona
Merchant con catalogo activo, equipo chico y necesidad de resolver un problema operativo puntual sin instalar una suite pesada.

## Flujo principal
Run a catalog scan, review duplicate groups, export or fix manually, and monitor updates later.

## Propuesta de valor
A bulk audit and warning surface for duplicate, missing and malformed SKUs/barcodes.

## MVP
- Dashboard for duplicates, blanks and malformed identifiers.
- Audit table grouped by SKU/barcode issue type.
- Settings for required fields and allowed patterns.
- Fixtures for duplicate, missing and valid product variants.

## Futuro fuera de alcance
- Webhook-maintained identifier index.
- CSV import preflight checker.
- Alerts on duplicate creation.
